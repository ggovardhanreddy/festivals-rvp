# 13 — Brand Identity (RVP Youth)

## Horizon Crest

The RVP Youth mark is an original **Horizon Crest**:

- **Unity ring** — community and belonging
- **Rising sun / horizon arc** — celebration, heritage, new days
- **Growth fork** — youth, friendship, future rising together
- **Apex seed** — continuity and hope

It is geometric, iconic at favicon size, and free of clipart village clichés.

## Wordmark

**RVP Youth** — modern sans tracking, readable alone without the symbol.

## Palette

Crystal-glass holographic spectrum (deep blue → cyan → emerald → purple → magenta → gold → orange) for glass variants; forest + gold for heritage light/dark UI; monochrome black/white for utility.
