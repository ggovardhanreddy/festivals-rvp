# Brand Identity — RVP Youth

## Logo

**Community Unity Mark** — six stylized figures arranged in a circle (orange, green, blue), each carrying hearts — unity, celebration, and shared memory.

**Wordmark** — `RVP YOUTH` in bold geometric sans, brand blue `#4A81BF`.

### Colors

| Role | Hex |
|------|-----|
| Orange (top figure) | `#E88B41` |
| Green (upper sides) | `#8BC367` |
| Blue (lower figures + wordmark) | `#4A81BF` |
| Cream (print / light grounds) | `#FFFDF0` |

### Assets

Generated by `npm run brand:logo` (`scripts/generate-logo-system.ts`):

- Master source: `public/brand/rvp-youth-logo-master.png`
- Vertical lockup: `public/logo/logo-vertical.png`
- Mark / favicon / app icons under `public/logo/`
- Horizontal SVG variants: light, dark, white, gold, glass

## Taglines

1. **Where Every Celebration Becomes a Legacy.**
2. **Our Village. Our Heritage. Our Memories.**
3. **Together We Celebrate. Together We Remember.**

Defined in `lib/site.ts` as `SITE_TAGLINE`, `SITE_TAGLINE_HERITAGE`, `SITE_TAGLINE_TOGETHER`.

## Usage

- Nav / footer: horizontal logo (`logo-light` / `logo-dark`)
- Cinematic intro & hero: vertical master lockup
- Prefer the community mark over previous Horizon Crest assets
